3D ex vivo human brain models

Human brain tissue, kept alive, to study the tumours that grow in it.

We cultivate long-lasting ex vivo human brain slice cultures that preserve the architecture and cell composition of the living cortex, and read them with single-nucleus sequencing, spatial transcriptomics and imaging. The aim is to understand glioblastoma in its native human environment and to turn that into diagnostics and treatments.

FINALE

An autograft, immune-competent human glioblastoma model

Patient-matched tumour and cortex in one culture, so the immune interactions in the microenvironment are the patient's own rather than an artefact of mismatched tissue.

CRYON's

Cryopreservation of human brain tissue

What freezing and thawing do to human brain tissue, measured over several time scales with sequencing and imaging, in a field with almost no characterisation so far.

IDH-mutant glioma

Reactive astrocytes in IDH-mutant glioma

The transcriptomic signature of astrocytes inside and around IDH1/2-mutant tumours, and how their inflammatory transformation differs from IDH wild-type disease.

CAR immune cells

mRNA-based multi-functional CAR immune cells

Engineered peripheral blood mononuclear cells tested in human slice cultures and a human GBM model, with Tobias Weiss, University Hospital Zurich.
