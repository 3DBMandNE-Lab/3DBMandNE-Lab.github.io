Glia · Glioma · Systems biology

Human brain tissue, kept alive, read at single-cell and spatial resolution.

We maintain long-term human cortical slice cultures and build the computational frameworks to read them. The aim is mechanistic insight into how glioma and glia interact, translated into precision neuro-oncology and intraoperative diagnostics.

What we do

All themes →
01

3D ex vivo brain models

Long-term, viable human cortical slices that preserve tissue architecture and cellular heterogeneity for glioma and neurodegeneration studies.

02

Spatial multi-omics

Single-nucleus RNA-seq, spatial transcriptomics and multi-dimensional imaging, integrated to map tumour microenvironments and cell–cell interactions.

03

Tumour–host interactions

Glioblastoma invasion, immune modulation and neuron–tumour synaptic coupling, dissected in organotypic models.

04

Clinical diagnostics

Molecular fingerprinting and machine-learning tools aimed at real-time diagnostics in the operating room.
