Principal Investigator

Dr. Vidhya Madapusi Ravi

Principal Investigator · ORCID 0000-0003-0062-2099 · full CV

Principal Investigator of the 3D-Brain Models Lab since 2023, after five years as junior PI in the Department of Neurosurgery (2018–2022). Postdoctoral work in the Section for Neuroelectronic Systems in Freiburg (2017–2018) and at the Leibniz-Forschungsinstitut für Molekulare Pharmakologie in Berlin (2016). Doctorate in biophysics and biochemistry at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (2012–2016, Cum Laude with honours, funded by a Generalitat de Catalunya FI fellowship and recognised with the AGAUR thesis prize), on the conformational mechanism of the melibiose transporter. Master's in bioinformatics and Bachelor's in chemistry, University of Madras, Chennai. FRIAS Junior Fellow 2021–22. Biological Safety Officer for the S1 and S2 facilities of the University Clinic since October 2024. Founder and chief organiser of the Neuron-Glia Interface meeting in Freiburg.

Reviewer for Nature Genetics, Nature Communications, Nature Protocols, Cell Reports, Communications Biology, Signal Transduction and Targeted Therapy, STAR Protocols and Neuro-Oncology Advances (ORCID peer-review record).

Join the lab

We are looking for researchers who want to work on human brain tissue: PhD students, postdocs and visiting scientists with a background in neuroscience, neuro-oncology, tumour immunology, tissue culture, or spatial and single-cell omics. The team is small, international and sits inside a neurosurgical department, so tissue, clinicians and questions are close at hand.

Write to vidhya.ravi@uniklinik-freiburg.de with a short description of what you want to work on and a CV.
