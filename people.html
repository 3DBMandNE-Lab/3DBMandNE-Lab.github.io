Principal Investigator

Dr. Vidhya Madapusi Ravi

Principal Investigator · ORCID 0000-0003-0062-2099 · full CV

Principal Investigator of the 3D-Brain Models Lab since February 2023, and junior PI in the Department of Neurosurgery from 2018. Postdoctoral work in the Section for Neuroelectronic Systems in Freiburg and at the Leibniz-Forschungsinstitut für Molekulare Pharmakologie in Berlin; PhD in biophysics and biochemistry at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona (2016, Cum Laude with honours); Master's in bioinformatics and Bachelor's in chemistry, Chennai. FRIAS Junior Fellow 2021–22. Biological Safety Officer for the S1 and S2 facilities of the University Clinic since October 2024. Founder and chief organiser of the Neuron-Glia Interface meeting in Freiburg.

Join the lab

We are looking for researchers who want to work on human brain tissue: PhD students, postdocs and visiting scientists with a background in neuroscience, neuro-oncology, tumour immunology, tissue culture, or spatial and single-cell omics. The team is small, international and sits inside a neurosurgical department, so tissue, clinicians and questions are close at hand.

Write to vidhya.ravi@uniklinik-freiburg.de with a short description of what you want to work on and a CV.
